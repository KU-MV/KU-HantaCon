PI Introduction

Professor Jin-Won Song is a Professor of Microbiology at Korea University College of Medicine whose research focuses on virology, genomic epidemiology, and emerging zoonotic pathogens. His work includes infectious disease research using diagnostic and next-generation sequencing-based approaches. HantaCon was developed from this research framework.

HantaCon About the Program

HantaCon is maintained by the laboratory of Professor Jin-Won Song at Korea University College of Medicine. Reference viral genomic sequences used in this program were obtained from the GenBank database. For questions regarding program operation and maintenance, please contact the Project Manager (Kyungmin Park, PhD; kmpark0131@korea.ac.kr). For scientific inquiries or requests related to the use of original genomic sequences or project-derived results in publications, please contact the Principal Investigator (Jin-Won Song; jwsong@korea.ac.kr).
